# Production tracker — steps to content

This ties it all together. Each build step from **STEPS.md** gets mapped here to a content angle, a format, and a status. Work from this table when you sit down to batch record.

- **STEPS.md** = the raw record of what I did.
- **CONTENT.md** = the deeper hook, teach, and receipt scripts.
- **PRODUCTION.md** (this file) = the bridge and the to do board.

## Status flow
`idea` → `scripted` → `recorded` → `edited` → `posted`

## Format
- **Short** = 15 to 60 sec vertical, one idea, fast hook.
- **Long** = 3 to 10 min walkthrough or screen recording.

## Batch recording tips
- Pick a row, open the matching CONTENT.md section for the full script, film it.
- Short form first. They are fast and you can shoot many in one session.
- Record the screen while you build the next step so you always have B roll.
- Update the Status column as you go so nothing falls through the cracks.
